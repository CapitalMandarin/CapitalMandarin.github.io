01 / The experience

More than a holiday. A real Chinese school experience.

Our primary school immersion program brings children into the everyday life of a partner school. Instead of learning Chinese only from a textbook, they participate in full school days, use Mandarin in meaningful situations and connect with classmates.

From classroom activities to school routines and shared play, children discover how language works in real life. Placement is matched to age and Mandarin proficiency.

Explore the two school options →

02 / A special time to visit

Why Easter is a great time to experience China

01

School holidays meet school term

Many overseas spring breaks fall during China's spring semester, creating an opportunity to experience regular school life without missing lessons at home.

02

China comes into bloom

Discover spring blossoms, parks and city life as Beijing warms up or enjoy Kunming's famously gentle spring atmosphere.

03

A meaningful family break

While children learn at school, parents can explore independently and enjoy family time together after school.

03 / Choose your destination

Two cities, two beautiful spring experiences

One immersion program, two very different ways to discover China.

EASTER IN BEIJING

Beijing · Spring in the capital

Where history meets spring

Peach blossoms along the Great Wall, magnolia flowers near historic architecture, royal gardens and lively hutongs. Discover the capital's rich heritage as the city awakens in spring.

  • Great Wall
  • Spring blossoms
  • Imperial heritage
  • Hutong life
EASTER IN KUNMING

Kunming · City of eternal spring

Spring in full bloom

Enjoy mild spring days, flowering parks, lakeside walks and the diverse traditions of Yunnan. Kunming offers a relaxed setting for families combining school immersion with time outdoors.

  • Mild weather
  • Flower gardens
  • Lakes & parks
  • Yunnan culture

City highlights are inspiration for independent family exploration, not included school excursions. Weather and flowering dates vary.

04 / Our Partner Schools

Fangcaodi International School

Experience authentic Chinese primary school life at Fangcaodi International School. Students have the opportunity to learn alongside local classmates, develop their Mandarin skills and experience everyday school life in an immersive environment.

Our Easter Primary School Immersion Program is available in both Beijing and Kunming.

05 / Learning pathways

Find the right class for your child

Two options, matched to each learner's Mandarin level.

International Division

Build confidence in spoken Mandarin

Suitable for beginners and children developing basic Chinese skills.

  • Multicultural learning environment
  • Chinese communication in daily activities
  • Chinese and English support as appropriate

Local Division

Develop listening, speaking, reading and writing

Suitable for learners with stronger Mandarin foundations.

  • Mandarin-medium school environment
  • Regular learning with local Chinese classmates
  • Authentic classroom and campus routines

08 / Application

How to apply

1. Tell us about your childAge, Mandarin level, preferred city and holiday dates.
2. Confirm the placementWe coordinate with the partner school to check class and dates.
3. Submit application documentsRegistration details, photo, introduction, passport copies and medical documentation as required.

We recommend enquiring one to two months before your preferred start.

09 / Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can my child join without speaking Chinese?

Yes. The International Division is designed for children developing their Mandarin, including beginners, subject to placement confirmation.

Can we choose our own dates?

Yes, tell us your school holiday dates. We will check the partner school's calendar and availability.

Are sightseeing trips included?

The city highlights describe opportunities for families outside school hours. Ask us separately about any additional arrangements.

Can schools or groups apply?

Yes, we welcome enquiries from individual families and overseas school groups.

How much does it cost?

Request a quotation based on the division, duration and any additional services required.
