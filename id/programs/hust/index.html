校The University

Discover HUST in Wuhan

Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) is a comprehensive research university located in Wuhan, China.

With academic opportunities across engineering, science, medicine, management and other fields, HUST offers international students a range of educational pathways.

Studying in Wuhan also provides opportunities to experience Chinese language, culture and everyday university life.

Undergraduate Programs

Explore undergraduate study opportunities in a range of academic disciplines. HUST offers Chinese-taught and English-taught degree options for eligible international students.

Foundation Program

Prepare for university study through a foundation program focused on English and academic subjects such as mathematics, physics, chemistry and biology. The program helps students strengthen their academic skills and prepare for the next stage of their education.

Graduate Programs

Explore postgraduate opportunities for students interested in advanced academic study and research. HUST offers master's and doctoral programs across different academic fields.

Chinese Language Programs

Improve your Mandarin through Chinese language programs at HUST. Study options may include longer-term language courses, short-term cultural experiences and Chinese language programs combined with internship opportunities.

问Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What programs can international students explore at HUST?

HUST offers undergraduate, foundation, graduate and Chinese language study options. Available programs and requirements vary.

Does HUST offer English-taught programs?

Yes. HUST offers selected English-taught degree programs. The available options and language requirements depend on the program.

Is the Foundation Program a Chinese language course?

No. The Foundation Program focuses on English and academic preparation, including mathematics and science. Chinese Language Programs are separate study options.

Can I get more detailed admission information?

Yes. Contact Capital Mandarin to discuss your interests and request information about the relevant HUST program. Formal applications and admission decisions are handled by the university.
